- 🔴 [[apt-remove|apt remove]] — Remove installed packages

- 🟡 [[systemctl-status|systemctl status]] — Check service status
- 🔴 [[systemctl-stop|systemctl stop]] — Stop a systemd service
- 🟡 [[ufw-allow|ufw allow]] — Allow firewall rule
